The Week

There's a pace here for everyone

Co-op Camp activities are designed for adults as much as kids. Whether you want to spend the week hiking, dancing, and doing crafts — or you'd rather sit in on a coffee talk and take a slow evening walk — the week accommodates both. Participation is always by choice.

Tie-Dye

The camp's most recognizable tradition. Create a spiral shirt or tote bag you'll keep for years.

The Potholes

A short hike to a series of granite swimming holes fed by a cold mountain creek. A camp highlight for generations.

Folk Dancing

A weekly institution. Callers guide you through every step — experience is irrelevant, enthusiasm is welcome. See the full dance list ↓

Talent Show

One of the most anticipated evenings of the week. All acts welcome, all ages on stage.

Hiking

Trails ranging from a gentle morning walk to a proper mountain hike — all within reach of camp.

Campfire Singing

Evenings under the stars with guitar, voices, and the kind of songs everyone somehow knows.

Crafts

A relaxed corner of camp for making things — from nature prints and candle making to friendship bracelets.

Sports

Pickup volleyball, softball, and other casual games. No team required, no score kept if you prefer.

Carnival

A beloved camp tradition — booths, games, prizes, and enough good-natured silliness to last the afternoon.

Coffee Get-Togethers

The unofficial heart of the camp day. Slow mornings, good conversation, and the kind of connection that's hard to find elsewhere.

Trivia

Evening trivia that skews toward fun rather than competitive. Teams form organically — grab whoever's nearby.

Auctions

A lively fundraiser for camp. Unique items, camp experiences, and enthusiastic bidding.

Group Exercise

Morning movement sessions — yoga, stretch, or light aerobics — to start the day in the mountain air.

Ukulele Strum-Along

Drop-in ukulele sessions for beginners and beyond. Loaner ukes available.

Current Events Discussion

A morning roundtable for sharing thoughts, ideas, and perspectives on the world outside camp.
